Referral Program Terms & Compliance

1. Qualifying Referral: A referral qualifies when a new individual or business client (who has not previously filed with KNA Legacy & Co. Financial LLC) is registered through our referral form prior to filing, completes their paid tax return preparation, and all preparation fees are paid in full.

2. Reward Payout: The $100 referral appreciation reward is issued within 7–14 business days after the referred client's tax return is completed, accepted, and paid in full.

3. Regulatory Compliance: In accordance with IRS Circular 230 and applicable federal and state tax preparer rules, referral rewards are strictly client appreciation incentives for introducing new clients and are never contingent upon a specific tax outcome, refund amount, or tax position. Self-referrals are not eligible.

4. Program Updates: KNA Legacy & Co. Financial LLC reserves the right to verify eligibility and update program terms at any time in compliance with applicable laws.
